      Methodology and limits

      What each trace does — and does not — say

      Measured milestone trace

      These points come from worker throughput output or recorded processed_total samples. They are expressed directly in work units per second. Their labels retain the tested fleet, worker mix, and whether M1 was paused.

      Java and initial Rust anchors

      The earliest persistence era has no stored processed-work counter. Its two anchors are therefore stage-cadence equivalent estimates: useful for scale, not controlled worker-versus-worker results.

      All-fleet stage-equivalent proxy

      For a six-hour window, the report sums each ordinary campaign stage transition’s nominal campaign.total_pairs and divides by 21,600 seconds. It combines active campaigns and fleets into one operational signal.

      stage-equivalent WU/s = Σ nominal campaign work per ordinary transition ÷ 21,600

      The boundary that matters

      The stage-equivalent proxy does not represent actual total fleet throughput: source: EXHAUSTIVE records the producer type, not full-stage completion, and early-adopt stages may contain only partial work. Historical per-stage processed counters expire; the durable processed_total counter has no timestamp history.
